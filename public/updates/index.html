Help Centre

Quick answers about installing and updating AutoBingwa.

How do I install the APK?

Download the file, open it, and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when Android asks.

Which version should I choose?

Choose the latest release for all features. Choose Lite if your phone has limited storage or is older.

Will I lose my data when I update?

No. Install the new APK over the existing app and your saved flows and history stay in place.

The app won't open after updating

Restart your phone, then open the app again. If it still fails, contact support below.
